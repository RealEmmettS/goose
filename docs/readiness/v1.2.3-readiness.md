Date opened: 2026-07-18

Target: stable/latest `v1.2.3`

Architecture decision: ADR 0034, retaining ADRs 0031–0033

## Intent

v1.2.3 makes the stable official PowerShell and shell bootstraps the recommended fresh-install
experience without weakening the provenance-preserving updater. Native DMG, MSI, EXE, and Debian
owners remain supported alternatives; updates stay on the proven origin; a deliberate fresh
official installer becomes the latest intent only through a verified transaction.

The patch also closes three concrete gaps: a fresh Mac shell invocation no longer silently keeps
an older DMG label, a fresh Global MSI no longer inherits an older PowerShell label, and Linux
shell/Debian cross-scope collisions stop before mutation with an exact assisted recovery.
Because old Mac binaries are immutable, the exact-tag script also recognizes their established
update-temp filename and preserves a receipt-proven owner without weakening ordinary fresh use.
