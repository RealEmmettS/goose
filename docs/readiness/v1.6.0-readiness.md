## Earlier failures and limits

Earlier native probes exposed a pre-map GDK application-id setter, incomplete private
desktop service registration and missing packaged compositor plugins. Production code
now applies the prop identity after mapping; fixtures use the distribution's normal
Plasma services and PipeWire ordering. No compositor permission bypass was enabled.
Intermittent earlier ARM note movement failures remain in their evidence; final-source
native qualification must pass the unchanged motion and expiry oracles.

Hosted software-composited desktops are distinct from physical KDE, Mac and Pi acceptance.
Historical Windows elevation acceptance remains open. KDE 5 supplies no stacking-order
authority for pointer control, musl does not dynamically load these pointer libraries,
and no DND or other compositor capability is inferred from KDE results.
